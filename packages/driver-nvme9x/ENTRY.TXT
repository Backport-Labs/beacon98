Warning: A wrong disk controller driver can stop Windows from starting and can
 damage data. Back up your data first. If Windows does not start, use Safe
 Mode (hold Ctrl or press F8 while it starts) and remove the controller in
 Device Manager.
Notice: The driver files are in {pf}\Drivers\nvme9x. Open Control Panel,
 System, Device Manager, open the NVMe controller (often under Other devices),
 choose Driver, Update Driver, "Specify a location", and enter that folder.
 Then restart. Drives over 2 TB also need SweetLow's LBA64HLP helper.
 Removing this package does not change the driver Windows uses.
