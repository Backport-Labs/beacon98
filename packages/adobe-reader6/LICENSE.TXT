Adobe Reader 6.0 - license
==========================

Adobe Reader 6.0 is proprietary software by Adobe Systems Incorporated,
distributed free of charge by Adobe. It is used under Adobe's End User
License Agreement, which the Adobe Reader 6.0 installer shows and asks you
to accept during setup.

The text of that 6.0 agreement could not be saved here: it is inside the
installer's compressed (Netopsystems FEAD) package, which cannot be opened
without running the installer, and Adobe's archive of older license terms
(https://www.adobe.com/legal/licenses-terms/licenses-terms-archive.html,
checked 2026-09-29) lists Reader agreements only from 2015 onwards.

Adobe's "Distributing Adobe Reader" page of that time
(http://www.adobe.com/products/acrobat/distribute.html, 2004 copy read at
the Internet Archive on 2026-09-29) said:

"Third-party Web sites are required to link directly to Adobe.com for the
download of Adobe Reader software. Hosting the software independently is
not permitted."

Distribution on CDs and intranets needed Adobe's separate Adobe Reader
Distribution Agreement. Backport Labs has no such agreement and does not
host this file. Beacon 98 downloads it from Adobe's own server
(ardownload.adobe.com / ardownload2.adobe.com). Read and accept the
agreement the installer shows before using the program.

Adobe, Acrobat and Adobe Reader are trademarks of Adobe.
