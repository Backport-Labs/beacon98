Package: wings3d
Name: Wings 3D
Version: 1.0
Section: Multimedia
Summary: Subdivision 3D modeller with a simple, fast interface
Description: A polygon mesh modeller inspired by Nendo and Mirai. It models
 with subdivision surfaces, has AutoUV texture mapping, and imports and
 exports 3DS, OBJ, VRML, LWO and other formats. Needs an OpenGL driver.
 Runs on Windows 98 and ME only with KernelEx.

Warning: Wings 3D 1.0 includes SDL 1.2.13, which has known security problems

 may also affect this version. Open only 3D models and images you trust.
