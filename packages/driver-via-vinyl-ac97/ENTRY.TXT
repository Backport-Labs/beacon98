Warning: Install this driver only if your sound chip is a VIA AC'97
 controller or your board uses a VIA Vinyl codec. On Intel, SiS or nForce
 boards with another maker's codec it may not work. A wrong audio driver

 in Device Manager to go back.
Notice: The driver files are in {dir}\Vinyl\drivers\WDM. Open Control

 Driver, Update Driver, and point Windows at that folder. Windows may ask

 Windows Installer 2.0 and a mixer panel) is in {dir} too.
