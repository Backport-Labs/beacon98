Matrox display drivers - license
================================

The Matrox driver package (w9x_433c.exe) contains no license text of its own.
The license Matrox now presents for its graphics drivers is the "Matrox Video
Software License Agreement" (document SLA03202026, 7 pages), which the
"Previously Released Drivers" page asks the user to accept before showing the
drivers:
  https://video.matrox.com/apps/video_drivers/_content/pdf/graphics_drivers_eula.pdf
  (linked from https://video.matrox.com/en/apps/drivers/graphics/previous)

Below is the start of that agreement and the complete END USER LICENSE part,
transcribed from pages 1-2 of the PDF on 2026-09-29. The OEM license,
warranty, liability and general terms (pages 2-7) are in the PDF above.
Read the full PDF before installing.
