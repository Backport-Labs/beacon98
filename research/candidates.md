## Classes

- **A** open source (licence named). **B** freeware whose own terms allow redistribution of the unmodified file.
- **C** freeware, redistribution not allowed or unclear, but the publisher still offers it (external link candidate).
- **D** out: commercial, shareware/trial, abandonware, Microsoft, legally risky, or the publisher no longer offers it.
- A trailing **?** means the class is a best guess that was not verified (for C?: publisher availability and terms not checked;
  for D?: believed no longer offered, not checked; for A?: believed open source, licence not confirmed).

Provenance: "(verified ...)" = checked on the publisher's/project's own page on 2026-09-29. "(memory)" = from prior knowledge, not re-checked.
"unknown" = not known and not guessed. The thread itself never mentions KernelEx or any other add-on (0 occurrences on the page),
so the **Needs** column is from verification/memory, not from the thread.
